# Grailio

Grailio is a collectible card app on BNB Smart Chain where every card is backed by a tokenized stock.

You open mystery packs to get card NFTs. Each card holds a tokenized stock inside it (a bStock, issued by bStocks), so a card is both a collectible and something of real value you own on-chain. There are 15 stocks, from NVIDIA and Apple to index funds like SPY and QQQ.

## How it works

1. **Open a pack.** Pick a pack and a risk level, then open it to reveal a card.
2. **Get a card.** Each card has its own art, a rarity (Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary) and the stock it holds.
3. **Decide what to do with it.** Keep it, stake it, trade it, gift it, or redeem it for the stock inside.

## Features

### Packs
Three pack editions, each with its own art and its own five stocks:

- **Fable** ($1): NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, GOOGL, AMZN.
- **Saga** ($3): SPY, QQQ, BABA, NFLX, EWJ.
- **Mythos** ($5): SPCX, ANT, SKHY, EWY, EWT.

Every pack comes in three risk levels:

- **Low risk**: every card holds the same amount.
- **Medium risk**: card values vary more with rarity.
- **High risk**: the widest range, with the biggest rewards for rare cards.

Low risk packs are on sale now; Medium and High open once the prize pool is funded.

Odds and possible rewards for each pack are shown before you buy. Packs are paid for in USDT, or in BNB swapped to USDT for you.

### Collection
All your cards and unopened packs in one place. Each card shows its status (staked, listed or unfilled), its rarity and what it holds.

### Staking
Stake cards to earn rewards, paid in USDT. Staked cards keep earning until you unstake them.

### Marketplace
Buy and sell cards with other collectors, priced in BNB or USDT, and filter by stock. Listings stay in your wallet until they sell.

### Redeem
Burn a card to receive the stock it holds. Redeemed cards stay visible on your profile as part of your history.

### Built-in exchange (DEX)
Swap the 15 stocks against USDT, USDC or BNB without leaving the app, so you can get what you need to buy packs or move between the stocks your cards hold.

### Gifts
Buy packs as a gift and share a link or code. The person who receives it claims the packs straight into their collection, even with a brand-new wallet.

### Community
Chat rooms for collectors: a shared lounge plus a room for each stock, where holding a card gets you in. Messages are stored on-chain.

### Profiles
Set a username, pick one of your cards as your profile picture, add a bio and social links, and share a public profile with your collection.

### DAO and points
Collectors earn points for taking part (one per pack bought), and the community can vote on proposals. Points can't be transferred, and there is no Grailio token.

### Demo mode
Try everything with a simulated wallet before connecting a real one. Nothing in demo mode touches real funds.

## Built on BNB Smart Chain

Cards, packs, the marketplace, staking, chat and profiles all run on BNB Smart Chain (chain 56) smart contracts. Sign in with a wallet or with email and social login. The contracts are live on mainnet, and anyone can connect. Stock prices come from Atlas Oracle on-chain.
